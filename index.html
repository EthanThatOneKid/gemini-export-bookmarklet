Gemini Export Bookmarklet

Exports the Gemini conversation you are viewing to a Markdown file, entirely in your browser.

Export Gemini Chat
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the button above onto the bar.
  3. Open any conversation at gemini.google.com, then click the bookmark.
  4. A gemini-chat-YYYY-MM-DD.md file downloads.
No network requests, no accounts, no data leaves your machine. Source: EthanThatOneKid/gemini-export-bookmarklet.
